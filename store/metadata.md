## 3. Detailed description
**URLComments** is a unique social annotation tool that turns every URL on the web into a shared communication space. It's like a treasure hunt for conversations that don't exist on the page itself!

Have you ever read an article, shopped for a product, or watched a video and wished you could see what others really thought—without being restricted by the site's own comment section? With URLComments, you can!

### 🌟 Key Features
- **URL-Based Comments**: Read and write comments tied exactly to the current webpage URL. 
- **Like & Dislike**: Upvote helpful insights and downvote spam.
- **Sorting Options**: Sort comments by Latest, Most Liked, or Most Disliked to quickly find the best takes.
- **Privacy First**: URLComments does NOT track your browsing history. The extension only accesses the current tab's URL when you explicitly open the side panel or click the refresh button.
- **SPA Friendly**: Fully supports Single Page Applications (like Instagram and Twitter) by restricting comments to their main domain, keeping conversations organized and preventing spam on dynamic paths.

### 🔒 Privacy & Tech
- **Secure Authentication**: Simply sign in with your Google account. No extra passwords needed.
- **Reliable Backend**: Powered by Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) to keep your data safe.
- **Zero Background Tracking**: Your browsing data is never sent to our servers automatically in the background.

Be the first to leave a treasure comment on your favorite sites!
